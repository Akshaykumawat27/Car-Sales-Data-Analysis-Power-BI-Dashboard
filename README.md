🚗 Car Sales Data Analysis Dashboard in Power BI 📊
Project Overview:
This Power BI project features a dynamic and interactive Car Sales Dashboard, designed specifically for car dealerships to track, analyze, and optimize their sales performance. The dashboard provides real-time insights and visually engaging reports, empowering dealerships to make data-driven decisions that boost sales and identify growth opportunities.

🔑 Key Metrics & KPIs:
📅 Year-to-Date (YTD) & Month-to-Date (MTD) Sales: Track real-time sales data for the current period.
📈 Year-over-Year (YOY) Growth: Compare sales growth with the previous year’s performance.
🚗 Average Price & Cars Sold: Monitor key sales metrics like average car price and total cars sold.
🔄 YTD vs Previous Year Comparisons: Analyze sales trends and spot growth patterns.

🎨 Visualizations:
📆 Weekly YTD Sales Trend: A line chart to visualize weekly sales patterns and trends.
🎨 Sales by Car Body Style & Color: Pie charts showcasing the distribution of sales by body style and color.
🗺️ Dealer Region Performance: A map chart to highlight sales by different regions for geographical analysis.
📊 Company-Wise Sales & Detailed Sales Data: Grid view showing detailed information about each sale, including company, body style, color, dealer region, and sales figures.

🚀 Tools & Skills Used:
Power BI: Built the dashboard with interactive visualizations and real-time data tracking.
Data Sources: Utilized CSV/Excel files containing dealership sales data.
Data Cleaning & Transformation: Cleaned and prepared data for efficient analysis using Power Query.

📝 Summary:
This Car Sales Dashboard provides car dealerships with a comprehensive overview of their sales performance, helping them spot trends, evaluate regional performance, and enhance their sales strategies. The dashboard is an invaluable tool for making data-driven decisions that lead to optimized operations and increased sales.
